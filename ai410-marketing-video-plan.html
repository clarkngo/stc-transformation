AI 410 Marketing Video: Plan

A video under two minutes that shows what it's like to take AI 410: AI Engineering, made from scripted screen recordings of the public student site. This page holds the decisions, the storyboard and captions, the production steps and the sign-off checklist.

Target length 1:45 AI voiceover, captions, light music Plays on this page; YouTube later Status: draft video ready for review
Standing rules for BSAI courses: the approved course description and outcomes are fixed, every hands-on unit runs in GitHub Codespaces, free tiers come first, and each HOS spans two weeks. See BSAI Course Design Constraints. The video only shows what the course already does, so nothing here conflicts with them.

Why this orderIt follows the same path a student takes in every module: preview, test the AI on purpose, build, then explain and refine by hand. The Term Project closes the story.

3. Production

  1. Record. tools/ai410-marketing-video/record.mjs drives Chrome through each scene at 1920×1080 and saves the frames. Every click and scroll is in the script, so a scene can be re-recorded in minutes.
  2. Edit. The recorder burns in each caption as a CityU-colored bar (Marine with a Blood Orange edge, Open Sans), and build.sh adds short fades and joins the scenes with ffmpeg. The closing card is closing-card.html.
  3. Voiceover. narration.json holds the script. add-voiceover.py speaks each line with Kokoro, places it in its scene and mixes it onto the video. Change the words or the voice there and re-run it. Setup is in the script's header.
  4. Captions file. add-voiceover.py also writes captions.vtt: the spoken script with its real timings, for upload to YouTube as closed captions.
  5. Export. MP4, H.264, 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 12 MB. A poster image comes from the hook frame.
  6. Upload. CityU's channel owner uploads it, adds the music from the YouTube Audio Library, and attaches the captions file.

To re-record after the site changes, from tools/ai410-marketing-video/: node --experimental-websocket record.mjs frames, then ./build.sh frames ai410-first-module.mp4, then ~/.cache/ai410-kokoro-venv/bin/python add-voiceover.py ai410-first-module.mp4 ai410-first-module-voiceover.mp4. It needs Chrome, Node 20 or later, ffmpeg and the Kokoro setup (about 1.1 GB plus the model), and takes about two minutes.

4. Rules for the video

  • Show only what's real. Every shot is a page students actually use. The Flashcard Coach example keeps its own label as an illustrative project.
  • No people, keys or personal data on screen. Recordings come from the public site, with no sign-in and no API keys.
  • Accessible. Closed captions of the full narration plus on-screen captions on every scene, text large enough to read on a phone, and no flashing.
  • CityU brand. The title card and caption style follow the CityU identity guide.
