CityUX

STC's non-degree lifelong-learning initiative — the open-ecosystem funnel, Open edX micro-credential platform, and the governance structure it runs under.

18 documentsJun 2026 – present
Also known as: AILI (AI Lifelong Institute) and Applied Intelligence Center (AIC) — earlier working names for this same effort, before the org landed on CityUX / CityU X. Docs under all three names are tracked here.

CityUX is STC's open-ecosystem lifelong-learning initiative: a staged conversion funnel (TOFU → MOFU → BOFU → Conversion) that moves learners from free YouTube/GitHub content through Open edX self-paced pilots and workshops into formal CityU enrollment, credit articulation, and micro-credentials.

The initiative's name and org structure have moved through several working titles — Applied Intelligence Center, then AILI (AI Lifelong Institute), landing on CityUX / CityU X as the president-facing framing. This hub collects the work under whichever name it was produced under, so the history stays traceable.
