Course description

CS 320AI 320 (AAC-approved, quoted word for word)
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to artificial intelligence (AI), covering both traditional and generative AI concepts and techniques. Students gain an understanding of core AI principles, explore traditional AI methods, and investigate generative AI. The course includes hands-on practical applications and discussions on the ethical implications of AI and the societal impact of automation. Upon completing this course, students will be well-prepared to incorporate AI into their major fields of study.Comprehensive introduction to AI covering both traditional and generative concepts. Includes hands-on applications and discussions on ethical implications and societal impact of automation.

Fixes to the source

  • TP03's “Data Security” criterion looked copied from another course's template; renamed to match this project.

  • HOS said “two quizzes,” but the grade table has HOS in all 10 modules; now “a short quiz each module.”

  • Not fixed: the MP rubric's Participation row describes replying to classmates (CD wording) rather than a reflection. Kept as in CS 320.
