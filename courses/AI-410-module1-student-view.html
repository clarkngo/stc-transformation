Overview
Welcome to Module 1

Goal: Build a full-stack chat app that can call a real tool — using an AI coding assistant to scaffold it fast, then proving, in writing and by hand, that you actually understand what it built.

This module is graded across four stages instead of on code correctness alone: Create/Scaffold → Evaluate → Analyze → Understand & Refine. You'll also complete a Muddiest Point reflection, a Concept Debate, and an Application Challenge this week — see below for each.

🎥
Lecture / Pre-work
LLM Architecture & the Agentic Loop
~20 min

Recorded walkthrough covering how a request/response call to an LLM works, what tokens and context windows are, and the agentic loop pattern — the model deciding to call a tool, your backend running it, and the result going back before the model answers. Watch this before starting your Muddiest Point below.

Discussion — Muddiest Point
What's still unclear after this week's lecture?
Due Wed5% of grade

This submission is open now and stays open all week — post as soon as you hit something confusing, don't wait until the deadline. Fill in all four fields:

1. What were you doing right before you got confused?e.g. "Watching the part of the lecture about tool calling…"
2. The exact place you got stuckBe specific — not "I don't get tool calling," but the precise line, term, or step.
3. What did you already try?Re-reading, a search, asking a classmate — show your attempt.
4. (Optional) Your best guessEven if you think it's wrong.

Instructor posts a short response addressing the most common muddiest points early in the week, before the Hands-On Skill build below.

💬
Discussion — Concept Debate
Should an AI agent decide on its own which tools to call — or should every tool call require human approval first?
5% of grade

You've been assigned a side below (not your choice) — small groups, staged across the week:

Stage 1 — Initial position (Mon–Tue): Argue your assigned side with a specific claim and evidence, not a general summary.
Stage 2 — Argue the opposite (Wed–Thu): Before responding to a classmate, post the strongest version of the other side's argument — the one you weren't assigned.
Stage 3 — Synthesis (Fri): Drop advocacy. State a genuine synthesis position, citing a specific point from a classmate's post — "great points, I agree" doesn't meet the Writing criterion.

Your assigned side this week: Group A — Autonomous tool calling. (Groups are ~5 students; you'll see your specific group and classmates in the discussion board itself.)

🛠️
Hands-On Skill (HOS) Build
HOS 1 — Foundations & the Agentic Loop
12.5% of grade~2–2.5 hrs

Full guide: HOS 1 Hands-On Guide → — starter code, exact prompts, and submission details. Four stages:

1. Create/Scaffold — not graded on its own. Prompt an AI assistant to build a full-stack chat app that can call a real tool.
2. Evaluate — test it on purpose (does it call the tool when it should, skip it when it shouldn't, fail gracefully on bad input?). Submit a short note on what you found.
3. Analyze — pick one specific piece of the AI-generated code and explain, in your own words, exactly how it works.
4. Understand & Refine — by hand, no AI: add a second tool yourself and confirm the agent calls the right tool for the right question.
🎯
Application Challenge
Design tool schemas for a different kind of business
5% of grade

Design the tool schemas (not full implementations) a completely different kind of business would need for its own agent, and justify each one — practicing the judgment call of "should the model be able to call this," not just the mechanics of calling it.
