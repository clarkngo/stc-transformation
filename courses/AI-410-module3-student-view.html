Overview
Welcome to Module 3

Goal: Make your app reliable under two different kinds of unpredictability: a model that sometimes calls a tool with bad arguments, and document ingestion that's slow enough to block the whole app if it runs inline.

Same four-stage grading as before: Create/Scaffold → Evaluate → Analyze → Understand & Refine.

🎥
Lecture / Pre-work
Probabilistic Logic, Guardrails & Asynchronous Processing
~20 min

Covers why model output is nondeterministic (the same input can give a different output), how a guardrail validates and retries against that, and why slow work (like document ingestion) needs to move to a background job/queue instead of blocking the request. Watch before starting your Muddiest Point below.

Discussion — Concept Debate
When a guardrail catches bad model output, should it retry automatically — or immediately surface the failure to a human instead of trying again?
5% of grade

You've been assigned a side below (not your choice) — small groups, staged across the week:

Stage 1 — Initial position (Mon–Tue): Argue your assigned side with a specific claim and evidence.
Stage 2 — Argue the opposite (Wed–Thu): Post the strongest version of the other side's argument before responding to a classmate.
Stage 3 — Synthesis (Fri): Drop advocacy. State a genuine synthesis, citing a specific point from a classmate's post.

Your assigned side this week: Group A — Retry automatically, up to a limit.

Hands-On Skill (HOS) Build
HOS 3 — Guardrails & Asynchronous Processing
12.5% of grade~2.5–3 hrs

Full guide: HOS 3 Hands-On Guide → — starter code, exact prompts, and submission details.

1. Create/Scaffold — prompt an AI assistant to add a validate/retry guardrail around a tool call, plus a Redis-backed queue and worker for document ingestion.
2. Evaluate — test a malformed tool call, confirm a document upload returns a job ID instantly and reports status correctly, and confirm chat stays responsive while a job runs.
3. Analyze — explain why the retry feeds the validation error back into the next attempt, or why the worker runs as a separate process.
4. Understand & Refine — by hand: extend the guardrail pattern to your second tool from Module 1.
