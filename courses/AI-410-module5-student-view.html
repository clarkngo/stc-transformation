Overview
Welcome to Module 5 — a pause, not a build week

Goal: No new build this week. Step back and look at all 4 HOS as one system instead of four separate weeks — diagram the combined architecture and find where it could actually break.

There's no Muddiest Point, Concept Debate, or Application Challenge due this week. That's intentional — it's a breather before the Team/Term Project ramps up.
