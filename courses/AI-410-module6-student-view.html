Overview
Welcome to Module 6 — the last review week before you build

Goal: No new build, no new tech. This week is for connecting all 4 HOS into one coherent system through the Concept Debate below, and for scoping or forming your Team/Term Project team before work begins in Module 7.

💬
Discussion — Concept Debate
If you had to cut a corner to ship on time, which of the 4 HOS is safest to cut — and which one can't be shortchanged?
Contributes to your 5% CD grade

This week's Concept Debate connects all 4 HOS together, not just one week's material. You've been assigned a side below (not your choice):

Stage 1 — Initial position (Mon–Tue): Argue your assigned side with a specific claim and evidence — name the actual HOS and the actual risk, not a general summary.
Stage 2 — Argue the opposite (Wed–Thu): Before responding to a classmate, post the strongest version of the other side's argument.
Stage 3 — Synthesis (Fri): Drop advocacy. State a genuine synthesis, citing a specific point from a classmate's post — "great points, I agree" doesn't meet the Writing criterion.

Your assigned side this week: Group A — Guardrails & async processing (HOS 3) is the safest to cut a corner on under a deadline.
