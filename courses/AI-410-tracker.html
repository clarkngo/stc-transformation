Design Review — September 23, 2026

Scope: a review of the 4 merged HOS for time on task, depth, rigor, and whether students can apply the skills on their own, checked against the official course description (fixed, per the BSAI Course Design Constraints). Time figures are estimates from reading the guides. No student timing data exists yet, and the first cohort should record actual time per HOS.

Findings

AreaFindingStatus
Grade weight The HOS guides and Module 1–4 student views said each HOS was "20% of final grade"; the syllabus and course page say 12.5% each (50% total). All eight pages now say 12.5%. Fixed Sep 23
Time on task Guides estimate 2–3 hrs per HOS. For students with no backend background, a realistic estimate is 3–5 hrs (HOS 1–3) and 4–6 hrs (HOS 4, mostly Expo setup: its guide lists 11 common snags). With the lecture, MP, CD, and Application Challenge, a typical Week 1–4 load is about 7–10 hrs. A 5-credit course usually expects about 15 hrs/week (assuming ~30 hrs per quarter credit; confirm CityU's figure). Weeks 5–6 have no build and are lighter still. Under the credit load
Coverage vs. description The description promises "best practices for deploying and monitoring AI models in production environments." Monitoring is taught (HOS 4, Langfuse). Deployment isn't taught in any current HOS: the app only runs inside the Codespace, and the Week 10 deploy guide belongs to the legacy schedule. Gap: content to add
Depth Each HOS merges two legacy weeks, and students work by hand on one mechanism per HOS. HOS 2's "Understand & Refine" is writing a Markdown document, not code; chunk size, top-k, and retrieval quality are never explored. HOS 4 measures a single metric (faithfulness), while O4 asks for "metrics" and latency/cost go unexamined. Thin
Rigor The CEAU grading design is sound. What undercuts it: Evaluate tests are prescribed, not designed by students; Analyze prompts often name the mechanism and hint at the answer; the "no AI" rule in Stage 4 can't be verified in an async course, and each Stage 4 task mirrors a pattern the AI just produced; Application Challenges are paper-only, with no unseen coding task before the team project. Moderate
Independent application The weakest area. Every Stage 1 prompt already makes the architectural decisions (file names, function signatures, embedding settings). Students learn to judge and extend a system someone else designed, but never design one, and nothing individually tests going from a blank repo to a working agent. Not assessed

Recommendations

#ChangeAddressesStatus
1Fade the scaffolding. HOS 1 gives the full Stage 1 prompt, HOS 2 a partial one, HOS 3 a spec only (students write the prompt), and HOS 4 the goal only.Independent applicationProposed
2Individual cold build in Week 5. A blank repo and a one-paragraph spec; build a small working agent in about 90 minutes. Fits the Week 5 checkpoint, which has no build today.Independent application, time on taskProposed
3Make Stage 4 tasks different from the Stage 1 pattern. E.g. HOS 2: change the chunking strategy and measure how often the right chunk is retrieved across 10 questions, replacing "write a sixth document."Depth, rigorProposed
4Honor code for Stage 4, backed by the Week 5 cold build. Each Stage 4 submission carries a pledge ("I wrote this without AI assistance"), tied to CityU's academic integrity policy. A second option lets students disclose AI use for partial credit instead of zero, so honesty costs less than hiding it. The cold build (#2) is the one verified check behind the pledge. This replaced two earlier ideas: screen recordings (grading load, easy to script, privacy and accessibility burden) and live spot checks (hard to schedule at scale).Rigor ("no AI" verification)Proposed
5Add deployment to HOS 4 or Week 5, to a free host that needs no card (e.g. Hugging Face Spaces).Description gapProposed
6Expand HOS 4's metrics to include latency and token cost from the Langfuse traces students already have.Depth (O4)Proposed
7Record actual time per HOS in the first cohort (a single self-reported field on each submission).Time on taskProposed

Recommendations 1–4 also apply to AI 420, which should build the gradual hand-off and a cold build in from the start.
