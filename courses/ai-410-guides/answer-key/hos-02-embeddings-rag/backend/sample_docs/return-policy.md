# Return Policy

Northwind Outfitters accepts returns within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided the item is unused and in its original packaging. Worn or washed items cannot be returned unless defective.

To start a return, use the return portal on our website with your order number. We provide a prepaid shipping label for domestic returns. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days of us receiving the item.

Sale items marked "final sale" cannot be returned or exchanged. Gift cards are non-refundable.
