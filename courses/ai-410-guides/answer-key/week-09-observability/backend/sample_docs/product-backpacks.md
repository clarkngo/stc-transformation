# Product Guide: Backpacks

The Traverse 55L is designed for multi-day backpacking trips, with an adjustable torso length, a ventilated back panel, and a rain cover included. Recommended load range is 30-45 lbs.

The Daytrekker 22L is a lighter option for single-day hikes, with a hydration bladder sleeve and hip belt pockets for snacks and a phone. It is not designed to carry loads over 20 lbs comfortably.

Both packs include a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects in stitching and zippers.
