AI 410: Hands-On Guides

Four step-by-step guides, one per HOS of the AI 410 schedule. Each guide walks through the Create/Scaffold → Evaluate → Analyze → Understand & Refine cycle: what you're starting from, what to prompt an AI assistant to build, and how to prove you actually understand the result.

Why This Matters

This isn't a tutorial project — it's the job

"AI Engineer" is one of the fastest-growing job titles in software right now, and it means something specific: not prompting ChatGPT well, but building the systems around a model that make it reliable enough to put in front of real users — retrieval so it answers from real data instead of guessing, guardrails so bad output doesn't crash the app, background processing so it doesn't freeze under load, and monitoring so someone finds out about a problem before a customer does. Every one of those is a separate HOS in this schedule, because every one of them is a separate skill companies are hiring for.

By the end, you have something to show, not just something to submit

Most course projects live and die on a grader's screen. This one doesn't: the Team/Term Project is a live, deployed, agent-driven mobile app you can hand someone a link to, built on 4 HOS worth of real components — RAG, guardrails, async processing, push, observability — not a tutorial follow-along. In an interview, "I followed a tutorial" and "I built this, here's why I made this specific tradeoff" are very different answers — this schedule is built to get you to the second one.
