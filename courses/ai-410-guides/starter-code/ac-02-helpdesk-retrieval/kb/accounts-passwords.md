# Accounts and Passwords

## Your Riverbend account
Every student gets one Riverbend account. It signs you in to the student portal, Canvas, campus email, and the library databases. Your username is your first initial, your last name, and the last four digits of your student ID, for example jsmith4821. You set your own password the first time you sign in at the student portal.

## Resetting a forgotten password
Go to reset.riverbend.edu and enter your username. A reset code is sent to the personal email address you gave Admissions, not to your campus email. Codes expire after 15 minutes. If you no longer have access to that personal email, bring a photo ID to the IT Service Desk in the Library, room L-104, and staff will reset it for you in person.

## Password rules
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. A passphrase of four or more unrelated words is easier to remember than a short complex password. You cannot reuse any of your last five passwords. Passwords never expire on a schedule, but you will be asked to change yours if it appears in a known data breach.

## Locked accounts
After 10 failed sign-in attempts in a row, your account locks for 30 minutes. The lock clears on its own; calling the Service Desk will not make it faster. If your account locks again right after the 30 minutes, a phone or laptop may be retrying an old saved password. Remove the saved password from every device, then sign in again.
