# Canvas Help

## Signing in to Canvas
Canvas is at canvas.riverbend.edu and uses your Riverbend account, with MFA. You see a course in Canvas once your instructor publishes it, which is usually one to three days before the term starts. If a course is still missing on the first day of class, contact your instructor before contacting the Service Desk.

## Submitting assignments
Upload files on the assignment page and press Submit. Wait until you see the green "Submitted" confirmation before closing the page. Canvas keeps every version you submit, and your instructor sees the most recent one unless they choose otherwise. Files larger than 500 MB cannot be uploaded; share a OneDrive link instead.

## Technical problems near a deadline
If Canvas is down or an upload keeps failing right before a deadline, email the file to your instructor before the deadline and take a screenshot showing the error and the time. The screenshot is your proof that you tried on time. Check status.riverbend.edu to see whether Canvas has a known outage.

## Notifications
You choose which Canvas notifications you get and how. Open Account, then Notifications, and pick email, text, or the Canvas Student app for each kind of notice. Announcements from instructors default to email right away.
