# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

## Why MFA is required
Riverbend requires a second sign-in step for every student account. Even if someone learns your password, they cannot get in without your second factor. MFA became mandatory for all students in fall 2025.

## Setting up the authenticator app
Install Microsoft Authenticator on your phone. Sign in at mfa.riverbend.edu, choose "Add sign-in method", and scan the QR code with the app. From then on, approve each sign-in by typing the two-digit number shown on your computer into the app. Text-message codes are allowed as a backup method but not as your only method.

## Getting a new phone
Before you wipe or trade in your old phone, sign in at mfa.riverbend.edu and add the new phone as a second sign-in method, then remove the old one. If the old phone is already gone, you will need a temporary access pass. Temporary access passes are issued only in person at the IT Service Desk with a photo ID, and each pass works for 8 hours.

## Stopping unexpected approval requests
If your phone asks you to approve a sign-in you did not start, tap Deny and report it. An unexpected request means someone else has your password. Change your password right away at reset.riverbend.edu, then report the request to security@riverbend.edu so the security team can check your account.
