# Printing on Campus

## Print credit
Each term, every student gets 300 free pages of print credit, loaded on the first day of classes. Black-and-white pages use 1 credit and color pages use 5 credits. Unused credit does not carry over to the next term. You can buy more credit in $5 blocks at print.riverbend.edu.

## Printing from your own laptop
Go to print.riverbend.edu, sign in, and upload a PDF or Word file. Choose black-and-white or color, then release the job at any campus printer by tapping your student ID card on the card reader. Jobs you do not release are deleted after 24 hours and do not use any credit.

## Printer locations
Printers are on every floor of the Library, in the Student Union next to the food court, and in the lobby of the Science building. The only printers that can print large posters are in the Library Makerspace, room L-020, and posters must be booked a day ahead.

## Refunds for bad prints
If a printer jams, prints blank pages, or prints the wrong colors, you can get your credit back. Submit the form at print.riverbend.edu/refund within 7 days and include the printer's number from the sticker on the front. Refunds are added back to your balance within two business days.
