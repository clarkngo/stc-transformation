# Connecting to Campus Wi-Fi

## Which network to use
Students should connect to eduroam. It is encrypted and works on every Riverbend campus and at thousands of universities worldwide. The network called RB-Guest is open to visitors, is not encrypted, and disconnects every 4 hours.

## Signing in to eduroam
When your device asks for a username, type your full campus email address, for example jsmith4821@riverbend.edu, not just your username. Use your normal Riverbend password. If your device asks about a certificate, accept the one issued to radius.riverbend.edu and no other.

## When eduroam will not connect
If eduroam suddenly stops working after you changed your password, your device is still trying the old one. Open your Wi-Fi settings, choose "Forget this network" for eduroam, and connect again with the new password. Devices with a randomized hardware address sometimes fail too; turn off private address for eduroam only.

## Gaming consoles and smart TVs
Devices without a web browser cannot sign in to eduroam. Register them at devices.riverbend.edu using the hardware address printed in the device's network settings. Registered devices can use the RB-Devices network in the residence halls only, and each student can register up to three devices.
