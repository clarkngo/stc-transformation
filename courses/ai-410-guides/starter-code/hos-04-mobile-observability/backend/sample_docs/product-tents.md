# Product Guide: Tents

The Ridgeline 2P is our best-selling backpacking tent, weighing 3.2 lbs with a freestanding double-wall design rated for 3-season use. It packs down to the size of a loaf of bread and sets up in under 5 minutes with color-coded poles.

The Basecamp 4P is a heavier, more spacious option for car camping, with a full-coverage rainfly and a vestibule large enough for gear storage. It is not recommended for backpacking due to its 8.5 lb weight.

Both tents include a 2-year manufacturer warranty covering material defects, but not damage from misuse or extreme weather beyond the stated rating.
