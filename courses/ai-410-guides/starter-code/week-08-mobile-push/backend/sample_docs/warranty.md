# Warranty Overview

Most Northwind Outfitters hardgoods (tents, packs, sleeping bags) carry a warranty against manufacturing defects — see each product guide for the exact term, since it varies by category. Warranty claims require proof of purchase and photos of the defect.

Warranties do not cover normal wear and tear, damage from improper storage, or damage from use outside the product's intended purpose (e.g. using a 3-season tent in winter conditions).

To file a warranty claim, contact support with your order number, photos, and a description of the issue. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at Northwind's discretion; we do not offer warranty refunds.
