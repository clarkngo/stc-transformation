# Shipping Information

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days and is free on orders over $75. Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) is available for a flat $15 fee. We do not currently ship internationally outside the US and Canada.

Orders placed before 1pm PT ship the same business day. You'll receive a tracking number by email once your order ships.

Canadian orders may be subject to customs fees collected by the carrier on delivery; Northwind Outfitters does not cover these fees.
