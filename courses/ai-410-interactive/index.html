Build a full-stack AI app, one module at a time

Ten weeks, five modules. In Modules 1–4 you build one app that gets smarter each time: an agent that calls tools, answers from your documents, survives bad model output, and runs on your phone. In Module 5 your team combines all four into a project of your own.
