About these pages

AI 410 is an upcoming BSAI course — these are sample materials, not yet offered to students. HOS 1 and HOS 2 are built out as complete two-week lessons, showing each activity (Lecture, Muddiest Point, Concept Debate, the CEAU stages, Application Challenge, Knowledge Check); HOS 3–4 lessons will follow the same pattern. All four simulators are ready. Everything follows the course design constraints: fixed official description and outcomes, GitHub Codespaces, free tiers first.

How every HOS runs — two weeks
Week 1 · Build & judgeLectureThree short parts, each paired with a quick check.
Week 1Muddiest PointFour-field structured reflection, due before the build. Graded.
Week 1Concept DebateAssigned side → argue the opposite → synthesis. Graded.
Week 1Create → EvaluateAI-assisted scaffold, then deliberate testing.
Week 2 · Explain & masterAnalyze → RefineExplain one mechanism; extend by hand, no AI.
Week 2Application ChallengeTransfer the judgment to a new scenario.
Week 2Knowledge CheckSelf-check quiz + the open questions.

Preparing for the Team/Term Project

The capstone combines all four HOS into one app (Weeks 9–10). Before Week 9, run your idea through each simulator and write one line per row:

HOSQuestion to answer for your projectSimulator
1Which 2–4 tools does the agent need, and which require confirmation?Agentic Loop
2Which documents ground it, and does the key fact land in the top-k?RAG Pipeline
3Which AI output gets validated first, and what's slow enough to be a background job?Guardrails & Queue
4What one event earns a push notification, and what one number goes on the dashboard?Push & Observability

Litmus test from the syllabus: can you point to a part of your project and say "this is HOS 2, and here's why it's actually retrieval, not a hardcoded FAQ"? Full framework: syllabus → Team/Term Project.
