Course design constraints

Your project follows the same BSAI course design constraints as every HOS: it runs in GitHub Codespaces, and it uses only free tiers — the same Gemini key, Chroma, Upstash Redis, Expo and Langfuse accounts you already have. Anything that would need a paid key or a billing account is out of scope.

What Weeks 9–10 are for

20% of courseOutcomes O1–O5Team or solo

The Team/Term Project combines all 4 HOS — the agent and its tools, retrieval over your own documents, guardrails and background jobs, and a mobile client with push and observability — into one app a real user would want. You've built every piece once already. The work now is synthesis: deciding how the pieces fit, building the combined version, and showing it working live.

There's no separate planning week. You've carried the idea since Week 1, checked it in at Week 6, and diagrammed the architecture in Week 8 — so Week 9 spends a day or two on a short backlog and then builds. Week 10 finishes, freezes, rehearses, and demos.

Days 1–2: product backlog and Sprint 1

Scrum-liteKeep it brief

Write everything the project might need as user stories — "As a [user], I want [capability], so that [benefit]" — and tag each with the HOS it depends on. Then mark a small, realistic subset as Sprint 1: what you can actually finish in the rest of Week 9 and Week 10. Everything else stays backlog, not a promise. Your list stays in this browser; copy it into your PM tool when it's ready.

Depends on:

Coverage

Sprint 1 should touch at least two HOS end to end this week, and all four by the Week 10 demo.

Litmus test for each HOS

For every HOS, can you point to a specific part of your project and say why it's really that HOS? "This is HOS 2, and it's actually retrieval over documents users upload — not a hardcoded FAQ." If a HOS only has a token mention, that's the Synthesis & Scope criterion at risk.

👥

Roles and a project management tool

Day 1–2

Teams (typically 2–4): assign an owner per area, not per week. One workable split: agent & tools (HOS 1 + 3's guardrails), data & retrieval (HOS 2 + 3's worker), mobile & push (HOS 4 client), and observability & demo (HOS 4 traces, plus owning the demo script). With fewer people, combine areas. Everyone still commits code — "I did the slides" isn't a contribution under the Collaboration criterion.

Solo: you own every area, so keep a daily log instead of standups; it becomes your process reflection.

Build: same Create → Evaluate habit, bigger scope

Rest of Week 9 → Week 10
  • Start from your HOS 4 app. It already has all four layers. Change what the project needs; don't start over.
  • Scaffold with an AI assistant, then judge it. Use the Term Project Prompt Builder to scope what you ask it to build — the same way each HOS's Create/Scaffold stage worked.
  • Get one thin path working end to end first — for example, one question that retrieves from your documents and calls one tool — before widening any single layer.
  • Keep Evaluating on purpose. For each story, one test that should work and one that should fail gracefully. Your Evaluate habits from Weeks 1–8 are what keep demo day calm.
  • Rehearse in the simulators when you're unsure how a layer should behave: 1 · 2 · 3 · 4.

Standups (or a solo daily log)

Async is fine

Brief, regular check-ins once building starts — a group chat is fine. The point is to catch "I've been stuck for three days" before demo week, not during it.

Standup — [date] — [name]
Did:      closed "upload notes as a background job" (HOS 3)
Next:     push when flashcards are ready (HOS 4)
Blocked:  Expo Go on Android won't receive push — need someone with an iPhone to test

Solo students: the same three lines, every build day. Together they become your process reflection.

Demo plan — show every HOS working

Presentation · 25%

A suggested shape for a short live demo. Adjust the times to your instructor's slot; keep the order.

~0:30The user and the problem. Who is this for, and what's hard for them today? One sentence each.
~3:00One live path through all 4 HOS. On the phone (HOS 4): ask a question answered from your documents (HOS 2) that triggers a tool (HOS 1); show a bad input handled by a guardrail (HOS 3); upload a document, keep chatting while it processes (HOS 3), and show the push arrive (HOS 4). Then open the trace (HOS 4) and point at retrieval, generation, and the score.
~0:45What didn't make it, and what you'd do next. Name the corner you cut — your Integration Debate synthesis in practice.
restQuestions. Expect "show me where HOS 2 is" — have the retrieval span ready.
🪞

Self-check and reflection

Collaboration · 15%

Self-check from the schedule: can you explain, without notes, how each of the 4 HOS shows up in your final project? Say it out loud once before the demo.

Reflection prompts

  • Teams: what you personally built (link commits or issues), one decision you argued for, and one thing a teammate did that made the project better.
  • Solo: your daily log summarized — where you lost the most time, what you'd scope differently, and which HOS was hardest to integrate and why.
  • Everyone: one moment where Evaluate caught an AI-generated mistake before it reached the demo.
