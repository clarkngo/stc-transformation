What this teaches

  • Reported ≠ true. A lenient judge inflates success rate; the gap only shows when you grade some transcripts yourself (agreement).
  • Teams cost more per task. The coordinator adds a routing call to every turn and a hand-off for every second topic. Did success go up enough to pay for it?
  • p95 tells the story averages hide. A few multi-hand-off scenarios drive the slow tail.
  • A simulator that won't stop pads turns, tokens, and cost — check transcripts before trusting the numbers.
