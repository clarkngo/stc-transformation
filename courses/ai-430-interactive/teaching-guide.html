The two-week rhythm

Same as AI 410

Every HOS runs the same two weeks as AI 410: lecture and Concept Debate early in the first week, the Muddiest Point due before the build, Evaluate at the end of the first week; Analyze, the hand-written Refine, the Application Challenge, and the knowledge check in the second. The day-by-day instructor table in the AI 410 teaching guide applies unchanged.

⚖️

Concept Debates

Same Brightspace debate teams and rotation as AI 410 (setup steps): Team 1 argues Side A in HOS 1, HOS 3 and the Integration debate, Side B in HOS 2 and HOS 4. This course's questions:

  • HOS 1 — version and track prompts like model weights, or treat them as configuration?
  • HOS 2 — deploy automatically when the gate passes, or have a person approve production?
  • HOS 3 — is an LLM-judge score a trustworthy production metric?
  • HOS 4 — may an enterprise agent act without human approval?
  • Integration — weak security or weak monitoring: which is the bigger enterprise risk?
📝

Grading

Same rubrics as AI 410

Same categories and weights as AI 410 (MP 5 · CD 5 · HOS 50 · Application Challenges 20 · Team/Term Project 20), and the same CEAU split per HOS (Evaluate 30 · Analyze 30 · Understand & Refine 40). Quick grading signals are in the AI 410 teaching guide; calibrate with the AI 410 example answers.

✋

Checking that Understand & Refine was done by hand

Same approach as AI 410 — no AI detectors. A one-line follow-up question, the Refine work as its own commit, "one thing your version still gets wrong," or a spot live check. Refine pieces here are small, checkable additions (a validation rule, a gate condition, a monitoring check, a guardrail callback), which suit the one-line follow-up.
