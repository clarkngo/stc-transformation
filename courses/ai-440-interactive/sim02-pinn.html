PINN Simulator

What this teaches

  • Data-only models fit the noise and extrapolate wildly. Watch the 4–8 s error with few readings.
  • The physics term holds the shape where there's no data — the PINN's sweet spot is sparse, noisy readings.
  • Wrong physics makes the PINN confidently wrong. Mis-set the damping and turn λ up.
