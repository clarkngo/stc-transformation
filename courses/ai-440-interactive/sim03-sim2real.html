What this teaches

  • Fixed physics trains a brittle policy: near-perfect in simulation, poor anywhere else.
  • Randomization trades a little sim performance for a lot of robustness — up to a point.
  • Too wide and the task gets harder to learn: sim success drops with nothing gained.
