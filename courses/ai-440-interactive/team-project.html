Team/Term Project: Plan, Build & Demo

Design and deploy the functional digital twin in Outcome O6 — for a smart building, a drone, or a manufacturing cell — with live data, physics-aware predictions, what-if testing, and autonomous decisions that stay inside a proven envelope.

Course design constraints

Your project follows the same BSAI course design constraints as every HOS: GitHub Codespaces, free tiers only. Anything that needs a paid key or a billing account is out of scope.

🧭

What Weeks 9–10 are for

20% of courseTeam or solo

The project brings every HOS together around one physical system (simulated): live sensor data driving the virtual model (HOS 1), physics-constrained predictions (HOS 2), simulation and what-if testing (HOS 3–4), and autonomous decisions that are logged and blocked when unsafe (HOS 4).

Example

One floor of a smart building: simulated temperature and occupancy sensors; a PINN for heat spread; what-if runs of an HVAC failure on the hottest day; an agent that adjusts setpoints inside the envelope those runs proved, monitored live in Grafana.

Backlog and Sprint 1

Scrum-liteKeep it brief

Turn each step into a user story — "As a [user], I want [capability], so that [benefit]" — and tag the HOS it depends on. Mark a small, realistic subset as Sprint 1. Your list stays in this browser; copy it into your PM tool when it's ready.

Coverage

Sprint 1 should touch at least two HOS end to end this week, and all four by the Week 10 demo.

Roles, standups and a PM tool

Teams: split by component — data connection, virtual model and physics, simulation and what-ifs, decisions and safety — and have everyone review the envelope. Solo: keep a daily log.

Rest of Week 9 → Week 10
  • Get live data flowing first. Everything else depends on the data connection.
  • Add physics to one prediction and keep the evidence that it helps.
  • Run the what-if study before writing the envelope; the envelope must cite it.
  • Log every autonomous action from the first day the agent acts.
