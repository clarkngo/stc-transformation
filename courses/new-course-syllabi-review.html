New BSAI Course Syllabi: Review

Draft, Oct 16, 2026. Syllabi for four new BSAI courses, each copied from an existing STC course (no time for new content) and laid out like the new AI 410 syllabus. Each course has a web syllabus, a Word file, and a side-by-side comparison with its source.

AI 251 Statistical Computing and AI Math (NS)

Source: CS 251 Statistical Computing

R → Python; Weeks 1–7 keep CS 251's statistics order, Weeks 8–10 add tensors, gradients, and backpropagation. Replaces the earlier AI 410-style draft.

SyllabusComparison.docx

AI 320 Fundamentals of AI

Source: CS 320 Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence

Nearly a renumbering. The LLM-comparison team project adds a traditional AI baseline; TP03's “Data Security” criterion fixed.

SyllabusComparison.docx

CS 352 Discrete Mathematics and Data Structures in Computing (NS)

Source: CS 351 Discrete Mathematics in Computing

Same chapters, re-sequenced to add hash maps, linked lists, stacks, queues, BSTs, heaps, Big-O, and Dijkstra.

SyllabusComparison.docx

AI 371 Cyber and AI Ethics (SS)

Source: IS 471 Cyber Ethics

Same chapters and assessments; each module adds an AI focus and free reading, five named AI case labs, and an Ethical AI Framework team project.

Rebuilding the Word files

The web syllabus is the source of truth. After editing a syllabus page, rebuild its .docx (needs pandoc and python-docx):

python3 courses/new-syllabi/build_docx.py            # all four
python3 courses/new-syllabi/build_docx.py AI-320     # one course

Styles, margins, and the page-number footer come from the AI 410 syllabus; the CityU banner is added at the top.
