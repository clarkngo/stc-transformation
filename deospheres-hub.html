DeoSpheres Partnership & AI Fluency for X

STC's work with DeoSpheres, a Seattle market-entry venture studio. The partnership covers AI fluency courses for communities (Korean American, Filipino American and healthcare, the trades), AI apprenticeships with Everett manufacturers, and Microsoft funding (Community First, Elevate).

10 documentsOct 2026 – present
Partners: Joneil and Sinae (DeoSpheres co-founders). Sinae's family also runs Morning Star Korean Cultural Center. STC leads: Dr. Sam Chung, Brian Maeng, Clark Ngo.

DeoSpheres brings international manufacturers and AI startups into the U.S. market, and it brings corporate, government, and community networks with it. The shared goal from the first meeting is an "AI Fluency for X" course. X is a specific community or workforce. The course uses STC's example-first, hands-on, concepts-last approach and runs on CityU's Open edX platform as part of CityUX.

There are three tracks so far: community AI fluency (starting with a Microsoft Community First grant for Korean Americans), AI in the trades (6-month joint engagements with Everett manufacturers on secure local AI infrastructure), and a longer-term Microsoft Elevate partnership. Related work lives in the Corporate Partnerships, CityUX, and Teaching Framework hubs.
