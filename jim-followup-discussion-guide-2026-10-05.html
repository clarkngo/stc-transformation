1. Agentic AI pilot — getting to a decision

Move from "interested" to "starting on a date"

Questions to ask

  • Of Project Leo, MBA 551, and BAM, which one would he be most excited to show someone in six weeks?
  • Is there a real deadline coming (board meeting, program review, Amazon conversation) that a first brief could feed?
  • What tools does he use today, and on which accounts: personal, CityU, or Habitat? Any policies he already knows about?
  • How hands-on does he want to be? Is the goal his own fluency, or getting work done for him?
  • What would make him call the pilot a failure? (This surfaces the real success criteria.)
  • Who at CityU should hear about the arrangement, and should that happen before or after the SOW?

What I can offer on the spot

  • Run a 10-minute live demo: one deep-research prompt on the space economy, fed into a brief template, with a citation check.
  • Offer to draft the one-page SOW myself (scope, hours cap, data rules, IP, separation from coursework) for his review.
Guardrail reminder: nothing paid starts until the written agreement exists and the separation from any course Jim evaluates is settled. Keep using public data only until an approval covers anything else.

2. EMBA and AI fluency — going deeper

Where STC's teaching approach can help most

Understand the course first

  • How is EMV 582 laid out: how many modules, how long, and in what format (live, async, hybrid)?
  • How does the Baldridge Framework shape it? Is each module tied to a Baldridge category, or is it the program-level lens?
  • Who are the students (roles, industries, comfort with AI), and what do they struggle with most today?
  • Who is the instructional designer, and what is their timeline? Where can AI challenges still be added without rework?
  • What is SBM's current policy on students using AI in assignments and assessments?

Bucket A (course operations): questions to sharpen scope

  • Which operational task takes the most instructor time right now: feedback, announcements, grading, or answering student questions?
  • What LMS integrations are actually allowed, and who approves them?
  • Where does he want a human always in the loop (for example, final grades)?

Bucket B (AI-fluency challenges): what I can bring

  • A sample challenge: break-even or cash-flow analysis done with AI, where students must check the AI's math and assumptions and explain any errors they find. The skill being graded is judgment, not the prompt.
  • A Socratic-tutor prompt for pre-class prep that asks questions instead of handing over answers.
  • A scenario knowledge-check template: ungraded, spaced, practice-first, matching the flipped-Bloom approach he described.
  • A short AI-fluency rubric (use, verify, disclose, decide) that could be shared across modules.
Proposal to test: "Pick one module. I'll draft the challenge, the tutor prompt, and a knowledge check for it by our next meeting, and you tell me what doesn't fit the executive audience." This connects to STC's teaching framework work.

3. Habitat technology — the groundwork before AI

Audit → requirements → data → process → automation

Questions to ask

  • Audit: Is the IT audit funded and scoped? Who does it (internal staff, Jim's contracted talent, or an outside firm)? When should it be done?
  • Current systems: What runs today for finance, the 99-year land trust, homeowner records, volunteers, and donors? Which are cloud and which are on-premise?
  • Pain points: Which process causes the most double entry or manual work? That is probably the first process-improvement target.
  • Data ownership: Who owns data governance, and what does it take to approve a dataset or tool for AI use?
  • Knowledge repository: Which staff members hold the most knowledge that isn't written down? Is anyone retiring or leaving soon?
  • Affiliate product: Which affiliates have said they're interested (Pierce, Snohomish)? Does Habitat International have standards or a preferred stack already?
  • Year-1 success: What does the board need to see in 12 months to keep backing the 3–5 year plan?

What I can offer (none of it needs sensitive data)

  • A systems inventory and requirements template that affiliates could fill in the same way.
  • A knowledge-capture interview guide for documenting what staff know. AI transcription and summarizing come later, once approved.
  • A public-data housing-policy monitor for King and Kittitas counties as a low-risk early win.
Clarify my role: ask whether Jim sees me as an advisor, a builder, or a teacher for his contracted talent. Also ask whether Habitat work goes through VMI LLC or directly through Habitat.
