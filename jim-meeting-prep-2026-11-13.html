Jim's five decisions

From Section 8 of the Nov 13 draft, with suggested answers

1. Sociomateriality as the anchor, and the "effective AI" definition?

Suggested: yes to the anchor; adopt effective AI as a working construct

Sociomateriality is common to both drafts. Effective AI is a strong organizing idea for Paper 2's outcomes. In Paper 1 it can be introduced and bounded without being tested. The draft is careful that the definition is the authors' own, inspired by a discussion at the Nov 10 session rather than quoted, so the paper should cite it the same way.

2. Keep CEAU central while broadening beyond business-school learning?

Suggested: central in Paper 1; the assessment instrument in Paper 2

CEAU is the only design case with artifacts ready (the case pack and Figure 1). Paper 2's outcome table already uses a CEAU rubric for "agency and judgment," so CEAU carries across both papers without having to be the whole story.

3. Which one learner group, unit, language, and device setup to test first?

Suggested: defer to the Dec 11 feasibility gate

No partner is confirmed, and the draft says community sites require locally defined needs, co-design, and consent. A controlled testbed first (an AI 410 cohort or a CityU certificate unit, as the draft itself suggests) would let the instruments be validated before any community work. Today: agree the criteria for choosing, not the choice.

4. What minimum effect justifies scaling, and what would falsify the assumptions?

Suggested: set in the Paper 2 protocol

For Paper 1, tag each proposition with the evidence that would confirm or falsify it (already in the Nov 5 charter). Effect-size thresholds belong in a pre-registered Paper 2 protocol, after a pilot gives realistic variance.

5. Which route and format are confirmed, and what moves to Paper 2?

Answered today; see Decision B

Everything empirical moves to Paper 2: the three-condition comparison, community sites, device and connectivity work, and the hypotheses H1–H4.
