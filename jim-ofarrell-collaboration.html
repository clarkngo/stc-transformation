Clark's email to Jim, Nov 13

Drafted Nov 13; not yet confirmed as sent. Subject: "CEAU paper: shared folder is ready, plus what I found on the ACBSP deadlines"

Hi Jim,

The shared OneDrive folder is set up: Sociomaterial CEAU Paper (you should have an invite with edit access). Start with "READ ME FIRST – Folder Guide," which explains where things go, how we name files, and our Thursday-post, Friday-review rhythm.

The ACBSP deadlines aren't what we assumed. I checked the official pages:

  • January 19, 2027 is the deadline for the main ACBSP Conference '27 in Baltimore (June 24–26), not the San Diego AI Forum. It asks for an abstract for a 30-minute presentation, not a full paper. At least one presenter has to be at an ACBSP member school, which SBM covers.
  • The San Diego AI Forum (March 11–13) has no public call for submissions. Its page lists only the theme and registration ($600 early-bird through January 31).
  • ACBSP's peer-reviewed journal, the Transnational Journal of Business, takes 5,000–7,000-word papers (APA 7). Submissions by January 31 are eligible for the June issue.

My proposal for the route: send the full paper to the journal by January 31, and an abstract from it to the Baltimore conference by January 19 (Teaching with AI track). That gives the manuscript twelve more days. San Diego becomes optional attendance. Happy to talk it through. I also couldn't find a New Orleans ACBSP event for 2027; where did that come from?

In the folder now:

  • Charter V2 (draft) in 00 Charter/Drafts, for us to approve.
  • Decision Log (top level) and Search Log (02 Reference Library), with draft inclusion and exclusion criteria for you to react to before November 20.
  • CEAU case pack (03 CEAU Case): stages, rubric, all four assignments, and the design evidence.
  • Figure 1, a first draft of the framework diagram (01 Master Draft/Figures). The four principles on it are placeholders until your theory section is in.

For the contrasting case, I'd suggest the AI Assessment Scale (Perkins et al., 2024), with the University of Sydney's "two-lane" approach as a second contrast.

To settle when we meet:

  1. Approve Charter V2.
  2. Agree authorship criteria. I've suggested the ICMJE criteria plus CRediT roles.
  3. Choose the publication route.
  4. Choose Zotero or a shared .bib file for references.

One more thing: the charter you sent Joel used the earlier "Beyond Bloom's Pyramid" title. Once we approve V2, it might be worth sending it to Joel so the review covers the current version.

Thanks,
Clark
