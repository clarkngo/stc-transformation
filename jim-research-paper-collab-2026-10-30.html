What Jim proposed

Summary of the Oct 30 email

Two research questions

  • How does a Create-first approach change the way students develop critical thinking, analytical reasoning, and genuine understanding?
  • How can we distinguish AI-assisted task completion from authentic student learning?

Theoretical frame

  • Sociomateriality: drawing on Wanda Orlikowski's work, which Jim explored in a Ph.D. dissertation. Human and technological agency are intertwined in practice, so students and AI jointly shape the learning process.
  • Co-Intelligence: Ethan Mollick's book, particularly Chapter 8, on learning and building expertise alongside AI.

The division of work Jim suggested: Clark's instructional design thinking combined with Jim's research background. Jim is open to other ideas on the title and direction.
