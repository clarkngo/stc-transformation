1. Effective AI: the working idea

From an advisory discussion the paper builds on

One advisory framing shaped the meeting. Effective AI is a course that works under the learner's real conditions:

  • Device: a learner may only have a flip phone. That's a delivery constraint (SMS, voice, a light web page), not a promise that a model runs on the phone.
  • Language: the language the learner speaks, checked by people from that community, not just machine-translated.
  • Context and culture: examples from the learner's own work and setting, the context where they learn best.

Jim's draft turns this into a construct for the paper: AI-enabled teaching that improves verified learning and learner agency under the real conditions where learning happens. It's the authors' own proposed construct, not an established definition.

No seminal paper defines "effective AI." A search on Nov 13 found none, so the paper should define it as its own construct and anchor each dimension in existing evidence. Candidate anchors, not yet read in full:
  • Basic phones: Angrist, Bergman & Matsheng (2022), SMS and phone-call support for learning in Botswana, Nature Human Behaviour 6, 941–950.
  • AI tutor on low-bandwidth devices: Henkel et al. (2024), a WhatsApp math tutor in Ghana, arXiv:2402.09809.
  • Generative AI tutoring with teacher support: De Simone et al. (2025), World Bank Policy Research Working Paper 11125 (Nigeria), World Bank OKR.
  • Evidence review: Walker & Vorvoreanu, Learning outcomes with GenAI in the classroom (Microsoft Research).

2. Choosing a pilot: five tests

From the session slides. Useful for Jim's decision on the first learner group.

Before a community or cohort becomes a pilot, the idea should pass all five:

TestQuestion
Real needDoes the community or learner group itself name the need?
Partner pathIs there a willing partner who can co-design and give consent?
FeasibilityCan it run on the devices, connectivity, and languages actually available?
Measurable valueCan learning be measured, not just uptake or satisfaction?
Next testIs there a small next step that can run within 30–60 days?

The slides also showed a three-phase concept roadmap (test, pathways, scale). It was labeled a concept only, not funded, agreed, or implemented, so the paper treats connectivity as a possible enabler, not a dependency.
