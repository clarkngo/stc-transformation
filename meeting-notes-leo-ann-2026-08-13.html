2. Strategy & Core Needs

Front-end portal, back-end AI support, and governance guardrails
Front-end strategy
Corporate Partnership Hub — a front-facing portal outlining tailored workforce upskilling/reskilling programs, employer benefits, and direct consultation booking for corporate VPs and directors.
Back-end / AI support
An executive-assistant-style AI tool/dashboard to streamline corporate contact tracking, prevent dropped leads, and manage automated outreach workflows. Future-state goal is to synthesize data across the systems below.
CRM
Microsoft Dynamics 365
Analytics
Power BI
Comms & Contacts
Outlook / Email & LinkedIn contact lists
Governance & risk: establish clear guardrails, policy frameworks, data safeguards, and compliance structures — addressing regulations such as the EU AI Act and state-level guidelines — before scaling enterprise AI deployments.
⚙️

3. Technical & Resource Constraints

Licensing, integration blockers, and available budget
Platform licensing
Standard accounts hit feature and token limits in Copilot Studio / Claude. Advanced enterprise features require specific administrative provisioning and seating assignments via IT/Leadership (e.g., Ben Phair or Brian Mear).
Integration blockers
Attempted Copilot Studio workflows currently lack native integration with the active CRM setup.
Budget
Business Development holds a flexible operating budget that can be targeted at technology/innovation tools when tied directly to enrollment and partnership goals.
