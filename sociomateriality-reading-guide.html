Sociomateriality Reading Guide

The reference library for our paper on sociomateriality, AI, and authentic assessment: 31 papers in seven clusters, each explained three ways (for a software engineer, a teacher, and a business reader) with self-checks.

What this is

A small test of effective AI, an idea from the Nov 13 meeting: learning that fits the learner and is checked by whether they actually learned. Every paper gets the same core (a one-liner, the argument, key terms), an on-ramp from your own field, a note on why it matters for our paper, and quick self-checks.

  • Where to start: Cluster 1 is the theory foundation. Clusters 2–4 bring it to AI and education. Clusters 5–7 add classroom evidence, sensemaking, and time.
  • Each page is separate so it loads fast on a phone. Your lens choice carries across pages; your check marks stay in this browser.
  • Three papers sit in two clusters each. They're explained once, with a pointer from the other cluster.
  • Each paper is tagged core, supporting, or peripheral for our paper, so you can skip the peripheral ones.
How this was made: Claude drafted the explanations from the PDFs, in our own words, and checked key facts against the text. No PDFs are posted; each paper links to its DOI or an open copy. Check anything you plan to cite against the original.
